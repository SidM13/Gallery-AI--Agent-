# Your Gallery AI Assistant

Your AI assistants work quietly in the background. You do not need to learn n8n
or use an approval dashboard. The messages that matter appear in Gmail.

## The one rule to remember

The assistant prepares drafts. **You review, edit, and send them.** A draft is
not a decision, commitment, price guarantee, or acceptance.

## When a new artist applies

1. Send the artist the application form.
2. Gmail will notify you that a reply draft is ready.
3. Open Gmail → Drafts.
4. Check the artist's name, tone, and proposed next steps.
5. Edit anything needed, then send—or delete the draft.

The application reply should acknowledge receipt. It should not promise gallery
representation.

## When a collector inquires

1. Apply the Gmail label `Collector Inquiry` to the buyer's email.
2. Wait for the “Collector draft reply ready” notification.
3. Open the draft and verify:
   - artwork is still available;
   - title and artist are correct;
   - price and currency are correct;
   - the reply answers what the collector actually asked.
4. Edit and send.

If any detail is uncertain, check the master Sheet or ask the artist before
sending.

## Monday opportunity digest

Every Monday morning, open the draft named “Monday Morning Opportunity Digest.”
Before forwarding an opportunity:

- open the original link;
- verify the deadline;
- verify location, medium, fee, and eligibility;
- decide whether it genuinely suits the matched artist.

Web listings change, so the original source is always authoritative.

## Friday gallery report

Every Friday afternoon, review the “Weekly Gallery Report” draft. It summarizes
artists, collectors, inventory, sales, opportunities, and upcoming calendar
items, then suggests three actions. Treat the actions as suggestions, not
instructions.

## Keep the system accurate

- Update artwork status immediately: `Available`, `Reserved`, or `Sold`.
- Add sales to the `Sales` tab.
- Keep artist names consistent across tabs.
- Do not delete or rename column headings.
- Tell the engineer if a notification repeats, a draft contains made-up facts,
  or a workflow stops.

## Privacy

The system runs its language model locally, but Google still stores the Sheet,
calendar entries, and Gmail messages. Enter only information the gallery is
authorized to retain. Do not put payment-card data, government IDs, or medical
information in the Sheet or inquiry labels.
